# Google Vids Assembly Timeline

Production kit for the 6 conversational interview episodes. For each episode:

1. Generate the clips in `pipeline/google_flow_prompts.json` via Google Flow (one 8-second clip per prompt).
2. Drag the clips into Google Vids in the exact order listed below.
3. Drag the episode's audio MP3 onto the timeline and stretch clips to fill the full 8–10 minute duration.
4. Export the final MP4 (1080p), then upload to Firebase Storage under `masters/interview-series-v1/`.

---
