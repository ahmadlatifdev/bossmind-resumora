## EN — Full production script

### Hook (0:00–2:00)

Situational and async interviews test thinking you cannot memorize. In eight minutes: handle prompts with critical thinking and emotional intelligence, master the two-plus-six timing pattern, and open every take with a brief, commanding introduction.

This lesson future-proofs Resumora’s Enterprise education package for campus cohorts and agency teams practicing recorded rounds.

### Body (2:00–7:00)

**Situational prompts**  
Clarify the goal and constraints aloud. Name stakeholders and risks. Choose a principle (safety, fairness, delivery). Walk Action steps. Close with Result and a check-in question. EQ: acknowledge feelings without losing the decision.

**Async timing (2 + 6)**  
Minutes 0–2: read, underline verbs, sketch STAR outline on paper. Minutes 2–8: speak. If the platform allows only one take, prioritize clarity over speed. Leave ten seconds of silence rather than filler.

**Commanding intro (20–30 seconds)**  
Name + role target + one proof line + agenda (“I’ll cover context, decision, outcome”). Then dive into STAR. Camera: eye-line at lens, shoulders square, notes below frame.

**Core messaging**  
Claim the decision quality they seek → Proof via structured reasoning → Relevance to their product/customers → Ask if they want depth on tradeoffs.

### Summary / CTA (7:00–8:00)

Practice one situational prompt with a timer tonight. Record your intro until it fits thirty seconds. Next lesson: global multi-language positioning. Enterprise admins: assign this module in team curricula when collaboration features ship.
