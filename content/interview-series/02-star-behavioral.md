## EN — Full production script

### Hook (0:00–2:00)

Behavioral interviews reward prepared stories, not improvisation. In eight minutes you will master STAR deeply, build four to five versatile story blocks, and answer “What is your greatest weakness?” with credibility — not clichés.

Resumora’s interview coaching layer (Enterprise education library) is built so teams can standardize these story banks across white-label and campus programs.

### Body (2:00–7:00)

**STAR deep-dive**  
Situation: context in one sentence. Task: your responsibility. Action: _your_ verbs — lead with decisions, tradeoffs, tools. Result: metric, learning, or stakeholder outcome. Keep Action longest; Result crisp.

**Story-block bank (4–5)**

1. Conflict / disagreement
2. Ambiguity / changing priority
3. Failure → recovery
4. Leadership without authority
5. Customer or quality save

Tag each block with 3 JD keywords so one story flexes across many prompts.

**Greatest weakness**  
Name a real, non-fatal gap. Show detection system (feedback, metrics). Show improvement action. Show current status. Never disguise a strength as a weakness.

**Core messaging**  
Claim the competency the question seeks → Proof via STAR → Relevance to this role → Invite follow-up.

### Summary / CTA (7:00–8:00)

Write five story blocks tonight. Rehearse each under ninety seconds. Weakness answers need systems, not slogans. Continue practice in Resumora; next lesson covers situational and async interviews. Enterprise teams: store story banks in your shared coaching workspace when enabled.
