## EN — Full production script

### Hook (0:00–2:00)

Welcome to Resumora’s Premium Interview Series. I’m your career coach for the next eight minutes.

Most candidates treat the resume and the interview as two separate games. They are one system. Today you will learn how ATS keyword alignment becomes interview ammunition — and how Resumora’s AI Keyword Tool turns a job description into a talking-point map.

Promise: by the end, you can walk into any screen with three ATS-backed stories ready in under sixty seconds each.

### Body (2:00–7:00)

**Block 1 — Keyword alignment (AI Keyword Tool)**  
Open the job description. Extract must-have skills, tools, and outcomes. Run them through Resumora’s AI Keyword Tool. Keep only terms you can honestly defend in an interview. Mirror phrasing on the resume without stuffing. Parsers reward clarity; humans reward ownership.

**Block 2 — ATS score → interview talking points**  
When your match score rises, capture _why_. Example: “I raised ATS alignment by reflecting cross-functional delivery metrics the role lists.” Convert each score lift into a STAR seed: Situation (role context), Task (gap), Action (what you changed on the resume _and_ in your work), Result (interview invite or measurable outcome).

**Block 3 — Project-first formats**  
Freshers: lead with 2–3 projects (problem, stack, result). Seniors: lead with scoped ownership (team size, budget, risk). Both profiles answer “Tell me about yourself” from the same spine: project → skill proof → role fit.

**Core messaging (CPRA)**  
Claim: I solve X for companies like yours.  
Proof: metric or project artifact.  
Relevance: map to this JD.  
Ask: invite the next question.

### Summary / CTA (7:00–8:00)

Resume and interview are one pipeline. Align keywords honestly, convert ATS gains into STAR seeds, and open with a project-first story. Continue in Resumora — Free and Pro unlock the tools; this Enterprise education library is the coaching layer for teams and white-label partners. Next lesson: Behavioral & STAR Method Excellence. Practice one keyword map today.
