# Resumora Support Policy (resumora.net)

## Plans

- Resumora sells one-time (lifetime) service plans: Basic, Pro, Business, Enterprise.
- Plans are not monthly Stripe Subscriptions.
- An active plan unlocks Resume Studio, Video Library (plan limits), and Client Chat.

## Access

- Only authenticated users with subscriptionStatus=active may use paid features.
- Password reset is available via /reset-password (Firebase Auth).

## Refund / Cancellation

- To cancel your plan or request a refund, open **My Account** and use the **Plan** tab.
- Enter a brief cancel reason, then submit. Your request is reviewed by our team before any refund is processed.
- Approved refunds deactivate plan access. Rejected requests leave your plan active.
- You may also request a refund from the **Transactions** tab for an eligible payment.

## Payment History

- To view your payment history, navigate to the **My Account** section of your dashboard.
- Open the **Transactions** tab for amounts, status, and dates.
- Open the **Invoices** tab for receipts or invoice PDFs when available.
- Payment history is private to your signed-in account only.

## Uploads & downloads

- Resume uploads are handled in Resume Studio.
- Video downloads appear under **My Account → Documents** when recorded.
- If an upload fails, try PDF or DOCX and ensure you are signed in with an active plan.

## Usage

- Do not share account credentials.
- Video downloads may have plan limits.
- Studio video generation requires an active paid plan.

## Contact

- Use Client Chat (paid members) or support@resumora.net.
- Transactional mail is no-reply from support@resumora.net.
