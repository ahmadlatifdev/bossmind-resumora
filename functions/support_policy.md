# Resumora client support policy

Public guidance for Client Chat. This file is the English source of truth for intent replies.
Do not include secret keys, price IDs, or payment-processor secret prefixes.

## refund

Paid plans may be reviewed for a refund based on how much of the service has already been delivered.
Open Account to review charges. Request a refund preview from Account when available.
Urgent billing questions: support@resumora.net.

## cancel

You may request cancellation from Account. Remaining unused work can affect whether a refund applies.
Cancellation does not delete your login. For help completing a cancel request, email support@resumora.net.

## studio

Resume Studio is the editor for your documents. Sign in, open Studio, and save often.
If Studio will not load, try a refresh, another browser, or a smaller file upload.
Still blocked: support@resumora.net with a short description of the screen you see.

## video

Video Library is for members with an active plan. Sign in, then open Video Library.
Downloads are limited per account. If a video will not play, check your plan on Account and retry.
Access still failing: support@resumora.net.

## human

A specialist can continue by email. Write to support@resumora.net with your account email and the issue.
Do not send passwords or full card numbers.
